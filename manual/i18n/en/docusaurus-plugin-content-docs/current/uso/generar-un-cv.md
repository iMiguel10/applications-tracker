Once your [profile](perfil.md) is filled in, the app lays out a PDF CV with the design you choose and saves it in [Documents](documentos.md), ready to send.

## Generate the CV

1. Click **Generate CV**, in **Profile** or in **Documents**.
2. Choose a **Design** (see below).
3. Check the **Name** it will be saved with. It is filled in with the design and the date; change it if you like.
4. Choose the **CV language**.
5. Under **What to include**, untick anything you do not want in this CV.
6. Click **Generate CV**.

The CV appears in **Documents** with the label **Generating…** and, after a few seconds, it is ready to view and download. You do not need to reload the page. If you generated it from **Profile**, the app takes you to **Documents**.

:::info Verify your email
To generate CVs you need to have [confirmed your email](primeros-pasos.md#confirm-your-email). If the installation does not send emails, you do not need to.
:::

## The designs

| Design | What it looks like | ATS-friendly |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic** | Serif, one column, no colour. For traditional sectors | Yes |
| **Modern** | Sans-serif, large name and navy headings | Yes |
| **Compact** | Smaller, denser type, so a long profile fits on one page | Yes |
| **Graphic** | Side band, large headings and levels drawn as bars | **No** |

Many companies screen applications with software (an ATS) that reads the text of the CV. The ATS-friendly designs use a single column and read in order. **Graphic** uses two columns: an ATS may read it out of order. Use it when a person will read the CV, for example when you hand it over in person or add it to a portfolio. When you choose it, the form reminds you.

## The CV language

**CV language** translates only the fixed headings ("Experiencia" or "Experience", "Formación" or "Education"…) and the months in the dates. Your content appears exactly as you wrote it in your profile: for a CV in English, write your profile in English.

## Choose what to include

**What to include** lists the sections of your profile that have something in them, all ticked:

- Untick a whole **section** to leave it out.
- Within each section, untick the **entries**, **achievements**, **skills** or **languages** you do not want in this CV. For example, for a frontend job you might drop the achievements that are only about databases.

Your contact details are always included. Whatever you untick is not deleted from your profile: it is just left out of this CV.

## A CV does not change when you edit your profile

The CV keeps your profile as it was when you generated it. If you later edit or delete something in your profile, CVs you have already generated do not change: they are still what you sent. For a CV with the changes, generate a new one.

## If it fails

If a CV cannot be generated, it shows the label **Generation failed** and the reason:

- **The CV could not be laid out**: try again; if it keeps failing, generate a new one with a different design.
- **The PDF did not fit in your storage**: delete documents you no longer use and try again.

Click **Retry** to generate it again with the same content.

:::info Limits
Each generated CV counts as a document and takes up storage, just like an uploaded one (see the [Documents limits](documentos.md)). You can generate up to 30 CVs per hour.
:::
