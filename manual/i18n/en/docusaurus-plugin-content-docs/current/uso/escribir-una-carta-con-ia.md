title: Write a cover letter with AI

The AI writes a cover letter for a specific job from your [profile](perfil.md) and the job description. You review it paragraph by paragraph and, at the end, the PDF is created with the same design as your CVs.

You don't need to [tailor your CV](adaptar-el-cv-con-ia.md) first: the letter comes straight from your profile and the job.

## Before you start

The requirements are the same as for tailoring the CV:

- the application has the **job description**, at least 200 characters long;
- your profile has at least one **experience**;
- you have [confirmed your email](primeros-pasos.md#confirm-your-email), unless the installation doesn't send emails.

The letter has its **own free use**, separate from the CV: using one doesn't use the other. It doesn't renew either, and it is spent as soon as the AI answers, even if the letter doesn't work out.

## Ask for the letter

1. Open the application and find **Tailor with AI**.
2. Choose the **CV language**: it is also the language of the letter.
3. Click **Write a cover letter for this job**.
4. If it is the first time you use the AI, accept sending the data with **Accept and continue**. It is the same permission as for the CV.

When the proposal is ready, click **Review**.

## Review the letter

The **Review the cover letter** page shows:

- **Greeting** and **Closing**, editable.
- Each **paragraph**, editable, with what your profile says that it is based on: the achievements, experience, skills, education or languages it cites. You can remove a paragraph or move it with the arrows.
- Paragraphs **Not based on your profile** are marked. That is normal in the first one, which talks about the job, and in the closing one. If one of them claims something about you, check that it is true.
- How many **words** the letter has. About 250 to 350 fit on one page.

:::caution Read every sentence
The AI can't cite achievements that aren't in your profile, but it writes freely and sometimes embellishes: it adds consequences your achievement doesn't state ("ensuring reliable deployments"), rates your level ("technologies I master") or implies experience you don't have. A paragraph being based on your profile tells you what it is about, not that every sentence is true. What you leave on this page is what the PDF will say.
:::

Your name, contact details, the date, the company and the position aren't written by the AI: the template adds them when the PDF is created, from your profile and the application.

## Create the PDF

In **Create the PDF**, choose the design (the same ones as in [Generate a CV](generar-un-cv.md), so the letter and the CV match), check the **Name** and click **Create letter**. The letter appears in [Documents](documentos.md) labelled **Tailored with AI** and, in a few seconds, it is ready. To record that you sent it for this application, edit the application and choose it as the cover letter sent.

Creating the PDF doesn't use the AI again: you can create the same letter with several designs.

## If something goes wrong

The messages are the same as when [tailoring the CV](adaptar-el-cv-con-ia.md#if-something-goes-wrong). The letter isn't discarded if your profile changes afterwards: only its text is used.
