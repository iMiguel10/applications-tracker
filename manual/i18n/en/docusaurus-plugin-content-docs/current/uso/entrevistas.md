Interviews are recorded inside the application they belong to.

## Add an interview

1. Open the application and, under **Interviews**, click **New interview**.
2. Set the **Date and time**. Everything else is optional:
    - **Duration** in minutes;
    - **With whom**;
    - **Type**: Screening, Technical, HR, Cultural, Final or Other;
    - **Format**: Online, On-site or Phone;
    - notes.
3. Click **Save**.

If the application was **Applied** or **Screening**, a notice appears: "Move the application to Interviewing?", with a **Change status** button. It is only a suggestion: if you ignore it, the application stays as it is.

## Record the outcome

Edit the interview and choose the **Outcome**: Pending, Passed, Failed or Cancelled. An interview's outcome does not change the application's status; that is done with **Change status**.

## Edit or delete

Each interview has its own **Edit** and **Delete** buttons. Deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.

Upcoming interviews from all your applications also appear on the [Dashboard](dashboard.md).
