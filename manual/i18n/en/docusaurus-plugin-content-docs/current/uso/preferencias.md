In **Preferences** you can change:

| Preference | Options | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Interface language | Follow the browser language, Español or English | In your account: it applies on every device |
| Flag applications with no activity after (days) | Between 1 and 90 (14 by default) | In your account |
| Theme | Light or Dark | In this browser |

Language and days are saved with **Save**.

The **theme** can also be switched with the sun and moon button in the top bar. It is stored in the browser, not in the account: you can use the dark theme on your computer and the light one on your phone.

The number of days decides which applications appear in the **No activity** block of the [Dashboard](dashboard.md).

## Change your password

In the **Password** card, click **Send link to change it**. You receive at your account email the same link as to [recover your password](primeros-pasos.md#if-you-forget-your-password): open it, choose the new password and click **Save password**.

The current password is not asked for: changing it requires access to your email, so someone with one of your sessions open cannot change it. Saving it **signs you out of every session**, including the one you had open, and you sign in again with the new one.

If the installation does not send email, the card says so instead of showing the button.
