## Create an account

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Create account**.
2. Enter your email and a password, and repeat it.
3. Click **Create account**. You go straight into the application.

The password must be **at least 8 characters long and include letters and numbers**. If the email is already registered, the application tells you: use **Sign in** instead.

## Confirm your email

When you create the account you receive an email to confirm that the address is yours. Click **Confirm my email** in that email: **Verify your email** opens and, if all goes well, it says **Email verified**. From there, **Go to the application**.

- You can use the application without confirming it. Meanwhile, a notice under the top bar reminds you.
- If it doesn't arrive or the link expires (it lasts 24 hours), click **Resend link** in that notice or in the **Email** card of [Preferences](preferencias.md), which also shows whether it is already verified.
- The link also works on another device, for example on your phone. When you come back to the application tab, the notice goes away by itself.

If the installation does not send email, you won't see the notice.

## Sign in and sign out

- To sign in, enter your email and password and click **Sign in**. The session is kept even if you reload the page or close the tab.
- To sign out, click **Sign out** in the top bar (on small screens, inside the menu).

## If you forget your password

1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send link**.
3. Open the email you receive and click the link. It takes you to **Choose a new password**: type it twice and click **Save password**.
4. You are back on the sign-in screen: sign in with the new password.

- The link **expires in 1 hour and works only once**. If it expired or you already used it, the page says so and offers **Request a new link**.
- Saving the new password **signs you out of every open session**, on any device. If someone had got into your account, they lose access.
- The application answers the same even if the email has no account: it does not reveal which emails are registered. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder and that the email is spelled right.

:::note No email
If whoever runs the installation has not set up email sending, **Forgot your password?** does not appear. In that case, ask them for help.
:::

## What you see after signing in

The first screen is the **Dashboard**, the summary of your job search. While you have no applications, it invites you to add the first one. The top bar takes you to the other sections: **Applications**, **Companies**, **Reminders** and **Preferences**.

Every account is private: nobody else can see your applications, companies, interviews or reminders.

:::info Interface language
The interface is available in Spanish and English. The access screens (sign in, sign up and password recovery) have the **Español · English** switcher at the top right, which is remembered in that browser. Inside the application that language is kept until you choose one in [Preferences](preferencias.md), which applies on every device.
:::
