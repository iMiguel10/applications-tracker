---
title: Archive or delete
---

## Archive an application

If a process is over and you don't want to see it in the list, **archive** it: open the application and click **Archive**.

- It disappears from the default list. You can find it with the **Archived** filter.
- **It is kept in full** (history, interviews and reminders) and **still counts** in the Dashboard metrics that describe what happened, such as applications per week or the response rate.
- To bring it back, open it and click **Unarchive**.

Archiving is almost always the recommended option.

## Delete an application

Open the application, click **Delete** and confirm. It is deleted **permanently**, together with its history, interviews and reminders, and no longer counts in the metrics.

The CVs and cover letters you created for it with AI stay in [Documents](documentos.md). And the free AI uses you spent on it **are not given back**.

## Delete a company

In **Companies**, click **Delete** on its row. A company with applications (archived ones included) **cannot be deleted**: delete those applications first, or move them to another company.
